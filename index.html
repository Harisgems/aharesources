Who We Are

One group. Nine enterprises. A single standard of care.

AHA Resources Pvt is a Pakistani conglomerate built on a simple conviction — that trust is earned in details. From tailor-made journeys across the Indonesian archipelago and the holy lands, to luxury hospitality, everyday retail, healthcare, aquaculture and global trade, every division is owned, operated and personally accountable to one name.

We combine trust, innovation and excellence to deliver sustainable business solutions and create long-term value across global markets.
