Legal & Policies

The fine print, in plain language.

Our commitments to your privacy, your money and your data — summarised honestly. Full documents available on request.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what a booking, order or application requires: name, contact details and the specifics of your request. We never sell personal data. Data is shared only with providers necessary to fulfil your request (airlines, hotels, couriers) and retained only as long as needed or as law requires. You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time via [email protected].

Terms & Conditions

Services across our divisions are provided under the confirmation issued for each booking or order. Prices are quoted inclusive of stated taxes unless noted. Travel services are additionally governed by carrier and hotelier terms. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited. Disputes are governed by the laws of Pakistan and addressed first through our customer desk.

Refund Policy

Retail, restaurant and service-division purchases: refunds or exchanges within the posted window with receipt, except perishables and opened personal-care items. Travel and hotel: refunds follow the cancellation schedule stated on your confirmation; supplier-imposed penalties are passed through at cost with documentation. Wholesale: credit notes for verified quality claims within 48 hours of delivery.

Cookie Policy

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Only functional storage (for example, your saved preferences) is used in your own browser and never leaves your device. Third-party embeds, if added later (maps, video), will be listed here with their own cookie notices.
