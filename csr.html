Corporate Social Responsibility

Growth that gives back.

Community development, environmental stewardship, education, healthcare and disaster relief — written into our operating plan, not bolted on at year end.

Five Pillars

Where our responsibility lands

Community Development

Local hiring first, village infrastructure projects, and support for artisans and small vendors in every district we operate in.

Environmental Sustainability

Reef-safe dive and snorkel codes, farm water stewardship, waste segregation across hotel, restaurant and retail outlets.

Education

Weekend classrooms, scholarships and school tour sponsorships — plus internships that rotate students through all nine divisions.

Healthcare

Subsidised medicine days through our Medical Store, first-aid training for staff and community volunteers, and blood-donation drives.

Disaster Relief

Standing reserve of food, water and medical supplies from our own shelves and farms, mobilised within hours of a declaration.

Responsible Travel

Community-hosted cultural sessions paid at community-set rates, group-size caps at fragile sites, and leave-no-trace operations.

Sustainability in Practice

Measured, not merely meant

Every division reports three sustainability indicators each quarter — water, waste and local spend — to the same board that reviews revenue. What gets measured at the top gets done at the counter.

  • Farms: pond-water recycling and zero-discharge targets
  • Hotel & Restaurant: local-first sourcing, single-use reduction
  • Travel: reef-safe operations and community-set cultural rates
  • Retail: dated-stock discipline to cut food waste
