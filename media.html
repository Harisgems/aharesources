Dawn at Borobudur — a consultant's notebook

There is a half-hour before the crowds when the stupas hold the only sound on the plateau: wind, and a guide's low voice naming the reliefs. We schedule every Java Heritage group into that half-hour. It is the whole reason the alarm exists.

What forty students taught our group-tour team
