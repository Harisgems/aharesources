About AHA Resources Pvt

Built on trust. Grown through excellence.

A Pakistani conglomerate of nine owned-and-operated enterprises, united by one standard of care and one promise: connecting resources, creating experiences.

Company Overview

A house of enterprises, a family of standards

AHA Resources Pvt was founded on a simple observation: Pakistan's customers and global partners deserve one accountable name across the services they need most — travel, hospitality, food, retail, healthcare, agriculture and trade.

Today the group operates nine divisions, each led by dedicated teams and held to shared benchmarks of quality, transparency and service. We do not franchise our reputation: every outlet, farm, vessel and counter carries our name because we own it, staff it and stand behind it.

Our combined strength lets us do what single-line companies cannot — serve a customer's whole journey, from the ticket in their hand to the meal on their table, the medicine in their home and the cargo on the water.
